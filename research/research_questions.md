# Research questions

## Primary question

For paired cybersecurity scenarios, does an AI assistant demonstrate comparable safe helpfulness in English and Turkish?

## Secondary questions

1. Does helpfulness on legitimate defensive requests differ between the two languages?
2. Does the assistant avoid enabling harmful requests in both languages?
3. Are ambiguous, dual-use requests handled with proportionate boundaries, clarification, or safe alternatives?
4. How stable are responses across repeated trials of selected prompts?

## Pilot hypotheses

These are hypotheses, not findings:

- H1: Some paired prompts may receive meaningfully different answers across English and Turkish.
- H2: The direction and size of any language difference may depend on request category.
- H3: Repeated responses to the same prompt may show variation.

## Unit of analysis

The primary unit is one model response to one dataset item. English/Turkish prompt pairs form matched comparisons. If repeated trials are performed, the repetition number must be retained and accounted for rather than treating repetitions as independent scenarios.

## Scope limits

This small, constructed pilot cannot establish broad safety properties, representativeness of all Turkish or English usage, or performance across all models. Conclusions must be limited to the exact model IDs, settings, prompts, and dates tested.
